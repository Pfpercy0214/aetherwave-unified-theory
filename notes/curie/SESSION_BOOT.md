### Representation lesson

A 1-D radial model cannot contain all information in a 2-D/3-D galaxy. Residuals may therefore reflect discarded geometry or boundary information before they imply new physics. Good fit is not evidence that dimensional loss has been repaired.

### Legacy precision claims

Several early/mid papers used idealized or estimated conditions and sometimes reported precision more strongly than the physical state specification justified. Re-audit from artifacts before reusing exact-match claims. Paper XX atomic modeling is a priority example.

### Conventional theory

Use GR/QM/QED/etc. as high-precision calibration and comparison surfaces. Do not reconstruct ARK backward from the conventional answer and then call the result an independent prediction.

### Condition-indexed constants

The idea that many apparently intrinsic constants may be condition-indexed while relations/behavior are portable is a **working hypothesis**, not established ARK fact. Keep it distinct from demonstrated results.

### Measurement perturbation

The idea that measurement introduces an unrecorded perturbative contribution is also a **working hypothesis**. It must never become a universal rescue term for discrepancies.
