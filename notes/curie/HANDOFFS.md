## Handoff format

When passing a result between agents, include:

- claim/result,
- status label,
- branch/path/commit if available,
- inputs used,
- fitted/calibrated/assumed/numerical freedoms,
- known null tests,
- unresolved alternative explanations,
- what would change the conclusion.

Avoid: "Curie/Gwok says X." Prefer: "Artifact X under conditions Y produced Z; Curie/Gwok interprets it as Q with these caveats."
