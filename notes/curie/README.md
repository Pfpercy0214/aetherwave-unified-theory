## Office rule

Do not copy large artifacts here merely for convenience. Point to their canonical branch/path. Keep this workspace small enough that a future session can load it quickly and recover the reasoning context without inheriting unnecessary noise.

## Rewrite posture

Historical papers are provenance, not automatic current truth. Modern subject papers should be reconstructed from audited concepts, relations, claims, derivations, and evidence. Cross-domain patterns should be represented explicitly as reusable relations rather than buried inside omnibus prose.
