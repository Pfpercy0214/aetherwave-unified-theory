## GR / QM posture

- GR and QM are **calibration surfaces**: high **precision of procedure**, not the mechanical substrate.
- They supply measurement-grade numbers and limits; they do **not** by themselves supply the medium mechanism.
- Aetherwave/ARK aim at **mechanism + geometry**; classical frameworks remain tools (telescope vs microscope metaphor on `main` README).

## DM / DE

- Dark matter / dark energy reframed as **phase regimes of a continuous medium**, not bolted-on exotic mass/energy species.
- Galaxy residuals: keep **projection loss (3D→1D)** and **outside / phase influence** as separate hypotheses with different falsifiers.
