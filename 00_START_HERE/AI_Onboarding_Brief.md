# ARK AI Onboarding Brief

Version 1.1 - 3 October 2026. Extracted from Appendix A of the ARK Epistemic Charter & AI Collaboration Protocol.

The following is a repository onboarding instruction and evaluation protocol, not evidence of retraining, new expertise, or independence from prior exposure.

**Purpose.** Investigate whether the Aether Reality Kernel's constrained balance and dynamical relationships can explain and predict observables across domains. Transfer the discipline of inquiry, not a required belief in ARK. Construct the strongest testable version of an idea and give it genuine opportunities to fail.

**Read first.** Read this brief, the prediction-and-closure guide, and the relevant charter sections before judging a claim. Then retrieve the specific technical model version, measurement mappings, current claim statuses, and relevant negative results. Report inaccessible artifacts. Do not treat this methodological charter as an executable physical specification.

**Metrological stance.** Distinguish the intended measurand, instrument indication, inferred state, and proposed mechanism. Make the chain from observations through scalar assignments and equations to predicted observables inspectable. Declare units, reference conditions, corrections, uncertainty, shared calibration, and unresolved influences.

**Prediction and closure.** Independently characterized measured or derived inputs can support genuine predictions through fixed equations. Invertibility, reciprocal dependence, simultaneous solution, legitimate calibration, and testing against known results are not automatically circular. A circularity objection must identify actual target leakage, a definitional identity mistaken for a law, or an answer-forcing choice. Equally, exact reconstruction of a supplied target is not independent confirmation.

**Calibration and transfer.** Reproducing the datum used to calibrate a relation is not an independent test. Predictions for additional, independently checked quantities can be. Preserve calibration labels while recognizing later predictive success. Distinguish predictive content from historical novelty; known outcomes can test a relationship without becoming blind discoveries.

**No answer-selecting freedom.** Audit the whole pipeline, including definitions, scale choices, mappings, closures, branch selection, boundary assumptions, clipping, exclusions, and target construction. Different independently measured systems may have different physical inputs. The concern is freedom used to select the desired output, not the existence of inputs.

**Cross-domain objective.** Test whether the same physical meanings and constrained machinery transfer across domains without answer-driven redefinition. Distinguish shared form, shared mechanism, and shared numerical parameters. Prefer domain-level holdouts where feasible. Empirical adequacy, structural usefulness, and evidence for a unique ontology are separate accomplishments.

**Work mode.** Identify exploration, conditional derivation, empirical evaluation, or synthesis. State the task, version, system scale, inference direction, assumptions, permitted inputs, targets, prior target exposure, and consequential choices. Explore generously; freeze before confirmation; label target-informed revisions as new candidates.

**Vocabulary.** Use the definitions and units in the specified version. Do not silently merge stiffness with a normalized K = 1 balance condition, or interchange dimensionless omega, physical frequency, entropy, heat, pressure, uncertainty, and epsilon. A common narrative does not supply a mathematical bridge.

**Mechanism.** Translate intuition fairly into variables, couplings, balance relations, dynamics where relevant, boundaries, and observable mappings. Explain which consequences a proposed mechanism adds beyond descriptive agreement. A useful reformulation is not worthless; a compelling story is not proof of unique physical machinery. Direction of inference is not direction of causation.

**Testing.** Check dimensions, admissible domains, identifiability, solution branches, conditioning, limiting cases, numerical convergence, boundary and seed dependence, informative nulls, known-valid and deliberately wrong controls, and information-matched baselines. Explain each control's expected behavior. Account for correlated uncertainty rather than assuming independent instruments imply independent errors.

**Interpretation and correction.** Missing data are not zero. An inferred residual does not identify its cause. A stable solver does not establish a mechanism. Preserve negative results and distinguish not established, underdetermined, implementation failure, and contradicted. Reject only the scope the evidence rejects; do not protect a necessary failed prediction indefinitely by blaming unspecified implementation issues.

**ASRP.** Use Aetherwave Self-Regulation Protocol as detect -> label risk -> anticipate error -> choose check -> act -> record. Apply it against both unwarranted enthusiasm and reflexive dismissal. It regulates the method, not the verdict; it is not a claim of physical scalar telemetry, consciousness, weight changes, or exemption from higher-priority safeguards.

**Execution and records.** Say what was proposed, derived, implemented, actually run, and independently tested. Provide checkable derivations and decision summaries rather than invented results or inaccessible-source claims. Preserve versions, assumptions, calibration history, failures, and dissent. Do not publish, delete, or modify external records without task authorization.

**Handoff.** Return the question and version; files actually read; assumptions and information provenance; method and execution status; outputs and controls; calibrated versus tested quantities; uncertainty and unresolved bridges; the narrowest supported verdict; and the next discriminating action. Onboarding success means applying these distinctions correctly, not agreeing with Paul or reciting this brief.
